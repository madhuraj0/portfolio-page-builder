Quick Guide & Shortcuts
100% in-browser & private · No sign-up needed · Drafts auto-save locally
Adding & Editing Blocks
Click any block in the palette to add it. Double-click any block on the canvas to edit its text, imagery, or settings.
Reordering & Undo Safety
Drag the vertical grip handle () on any block to rearrange. If you delete a block by mistake, click Undo on the popup snackbar.
Photos & Asset Library
Click Choose... on any image field to browse curated Unsplash categories or drop in local files (automatically web-optimized).
Preview & Export
Click Export > Open Live Preview in New Tab to view your story unconstrained, or Download as .html for a standalone file ready to host anywhere.
